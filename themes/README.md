# iDroid visual assets

The theme blends iOS 18-inspired clarity with selected iOS 26-inspired translucent materials.

## Asset policy

Use original or appropriately licensed assets. Do not bundle Apple's proprietary wallpapers, icons, sounds, fonts or other protected assets without permission.

## Planned asset groups

- `icons/`: original rounded icon family and adaptive icon sources.
- `wallpapers/`: original device wallpapers in multiple aspect ratios.
- `fonts/`: licensed typefaces and font licensing notices.
- `sounds/`: original notification and interface sounds.
- `tokens/`: shared color, spacing, radius and motion definitions.

## Visual principles

- Keep primary controls readable over translucent backgrounds.
- Provide opaque fallbacks for performance and accessibility.
- Respect Android dark mode and reduced-animation preferences.
- Avoid excessive blur on the Snapdragon 450 / Adreno 506 target.
